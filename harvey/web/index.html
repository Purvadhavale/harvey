Help

How the lead finder works, and what each label means.

How it works

1. Find brands      Google search per industry per city (Serper credits)
2. Read websites    phone, socials, Meta / Google ads, Shopify, hiring (free)
3. Team pages       named people + inboxes on the brand's own site
4. LinkedIn search  marketing heads via public LinkedIn profiles
5. Re-check         guessed emails, after verifier credits reset
6. Fix invalid      other email formats for named people

Results land in Brands and People. Export from either page. No email is ever written or sent.

Email labels

verifiedThe mailbox exists. Safe to use.
riskyThe domain accepts every address (catch-all), so it can't be confirmed.
guessBuilt from the name and domain pattern, not checked yet. Run step 5.
invalidThe mailbox does not exist. Run step 6 to try other formats.

Ad labels

Meta ads and Google ads mean the brand's website carries the Meta Pixel or a Google Ads tag — a strong sign it is spending on ads right now. Not checked means step 2 hasn't read that site yet.
